# Research status — 2026-10-11

Graph PR #2 and fixed-TU PR #3 are merged into main. Their independent
acceptance audits, original proofs, and source-pinned receipts remain
accepted evidence.

The new contribution closes the regular-matroid TU representation gate.
For two full-row-rank TU matrices of the same ordinary matroid, aligned
by a specified label isomorphism, a unit-basis normal-form proof constructs
an integral unimodular row change and a signed column permutation.
One verified witness transports all modular bicycle modules, both
modulus maps, and the Gram torsion identifications simultaneously.

ABC-REGULAR-REPRESENTATION-INVARIANCE records this new proved result.
It asserts independence of the tower's isomorphism class under TU
representations. It does not assert equal embedded edge sets or a unique
comparison map; explicit negation controls show why those are different
claims.

Eleven new tests take the combined suite from 41 to 52. The new receipt
covers nine TU pairs, 46 modulus cases, and 57 proper tower pairs.
The complete 729-matrix ternary census yields 456 full-row-rank TU
representations in seven labeled matroid buckets, all checked against
a separate signed-search oracle at four moduli. The 42 cross-bucket
comparisons are rejected. Graphic and cographic fixtures change Gram
coordinates and exercise noncyclic composite-modulus torsion.

ABC-MAIN and ABC-REALIZATION-INVARIANCE remain open. The universal
finite-group strengthening remains retracted. Non-TU integer matrices
with the same ordinary matroid need not have the same tower; they must
not be conflated with this TU theorem or with equality of arithmetic
matroids.

The next blocking target is Phase 4: exact equality of representable
arithmetic matroids using ranks and lattice-index multiplicities,
versioned fixtures, and negative controls. Bounded realization searches
should begin only after that comparison boundary is sound.

Python acceptance authority remains false. Earlier dated status records
remain historical snapshots.
