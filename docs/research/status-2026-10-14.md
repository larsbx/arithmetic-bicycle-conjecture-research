# Research status — 2026-10-14

Regular representation-independence PR #4 is merged. Its graph,
fixed-TU, and regular TU results remain proved.

The arithmetic comparison milestone exposes a refutation of the
original Arithmetic Bicycle Conjecture. The essential realizations

$$
A=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&5\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
A'=\begin{pmatrix}1&2\\0&5\end{pmatrix}
$$

have the same complete labeled arithmetic data: subset ranks 0,1,1,2
and multiplicities 1,1,1,5. Their ambient groups are free, so they
satisfy the stated torsion-free hypothesis. They are nonsurjective.

At both moduli 5 and 10, A has only the zero bicycle, while A' has
a cyclic bicycle module of order five. Direct cut/cycle congruences prove
this discrepancy. Therefore their compatible towers cannot be isomorphic.
ABC-MAIN and ABC-REALIZATION-INVARIANCE are retracted, with a separate
proved counterexample record. This is stronger than the earlier A=[2]
torsion-group-model obstruction, which remains a distinct valid result.

The new exact comparison kernel verifies integer Smith certificates,
computes rank/index multiplicities for every subset, and compares complete
data under a prescribed label bijection. A separate all-minors oracle
cross-checks those data; another lattice-index criterion independently
checks modular cuts over composite rings.

Thirteen new tests bring the combined suite to 65. Evidence includes
14 realizations, 91 subset records, six labeled comparisons, and 20
bicycle cases. The Smith census checks 15,625 integer 2-by-3 matrices.
TU specialization verifies unit multiplicity on all 648 subsets of the
original seven fixtures. The four-subset counterexample table has a
separate elementary proof, so the refutation does not depend on a
bounded census or an unproved Gram-torsion map.

The next target is a corrected statement, beginning with an audit of
surjective essential arithmetic representations. Surjectivity, or finer
realization data, must be named as new hypotheses rather than silently
inserted into the withdrawn conjecture. An intrinsic construction from
ordinary arithmetic-matroid data cannot recover the original tower on
the full stated domain.
