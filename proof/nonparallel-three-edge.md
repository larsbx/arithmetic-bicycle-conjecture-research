# Beyond the triangular family: a simple rank-two three-edge counterexample

**Status:** exact elementary calculation with bounded reproducible evidence;
research work under ABC-RD-01. No global nonsurjective classification,
no formal Lean proof, and no claim-registry promotion are asserted.

Fix b>=2 and parameters a with gcd(a,b)=gcd(a+1,b)=1. In the
declared ambient lattice Z^2 consider the three **labeled** columns

    A(a,b) = [[1, a, a+1],
              [0, b, b  ]].

Every singleton is primitive, and every pair of columns is independent
with determinant of absolute value b: the minors are b, b, -b.
Hence every admissible parameter at a fixed b has the same complete
labeled arithmetic data: the empty subset has (rank,multiplicity) (0,1);
singletons have (1,1); pairs and the full set have (2,b).
All three columns are pairwise nonparallel over Q. In particular
m(E)=b>1; no pair here lies in the surjective theorem's domain.

At modulus n=b, the second row vanishes, so the cut code is the
cyclic scalar line generated by w_a=(1,a,a+1) in (Z/b)^3.
A multiple s*w_a is a cycle iff s*(w_a.w_a)=0 mod b,
since the second row equation is zero. The self-dot-product is

    1+a²+(a+1)² = 2(1+a+a²).

The first coordinate of w_a is 1, so s->s*w_a is injective.
Thus the **exact order** of the modulus-b bicycle group is

    |Bic_b(A(a,b))| = gcd(b, 2(1+a+a²)).

This order formula does not establish a classification of all tower maps.

## New nonparallel realization dependence

Take b=7 and a=1 versus a=2:

    A1 = [[1,1,2], [0,7,7]]
    A2 = [[1,2,3], [0,7,7]].

They share the complete signature stated above. Modulo 7 their cut
generators are w1=(1,1,2) and w2=(1,2,3), with self-dot-products
6 and 14=0 mod 7. Therefore

    Bic_7(A1)=0,   Bic_7(A2) ~= Z/7.

The source-pinned experiment additionally checks their bicycle orders
at n=2,7,14,49 respectively:

    A1: 1,1,1,7;   A2: 1,7,7,7.

The order agreement at 49 does not erase the differences at 7 and 14.
The finite checks exercise reduction and *scaled* inflation for
2|14, 7|14, 7|49 and include a separate edge-space congruence oracle
at n<=14. This gives a second counterexample shape, beyond the square
two-column triangular family, without resurrecting the original claims.

## Pairing mechanism and next work

Let T=[[1,a],[0,b]] and B=[[1,0,1],[0,1,1]], so A=T B.
The standard dot-product on the three edge coordinates pulls back
along B^T to the nonidentity Gram tensor

    B B^T = [[2,1],[1,2]].

Consequently a change of edge presentation is not innocuous: the
bicycle tests are sensitive to the embedded row code together with
its pairing, not just to abstract arithmetic multiplicity data.
The nonsurjective Gram-cokernel torsion model is *not* invoked.

The bounded scan in experiments/three_edge_family.py covers b=3..13,
admissible a in 0..b-1, and the finite modulus n=b. Its reported
orders are exactly reproducible but are not an exhaustive
classification of representations.
