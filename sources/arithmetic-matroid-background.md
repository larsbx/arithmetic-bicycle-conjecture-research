# Arithmetic multiplicities and rectangular Smith forms

Definitions and source hypotheses checked on 2026-10-14.

Roberto Pagaria and Giovanni Paolini, *Representations of torsion-free
arithmetic matroids*, [arXiv:1908.04137](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1908.04137),
Section 2, Definitions 2.4–2.5: a represented subset's multiplicity is
the torsion order of the ambient group modulo its generated subgroup.
Essential means ambient rank equals total matroid rank. Torsion-free
means m(empty)=1; surjective additionally means m(E)=1.

The [local counterexample](../proof/arithmetic-realization-counterexample.md)
uses essential free ambient lattices with m(E)=5, within the originally
stated torsion-free domain. Arithmetic equality and equivalence of
realizations are distinct questions.

Richard P. Stanley, *Smith Normal Form in Combinatorics*,
[arXiv:1602.00166](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1602.00166), Theorems 2.3–2.4:
integer Smith factors describe quotient torsion, and products of initial
factors are determinantal-divisor gcds for rectangular matrices.
The [local proof](../proof/arithmetic-matroid-signature.md) gives the
saturation-index argument and an independent modular membership criterion.

These sources establish conventions and provenance. The elementary
two-matrix counterexample and the exact equality gate are proved in
the repository; no universal torsion or realization theorem is imported.
