# Source audit for TU representation equivalence

Checked 2026-10-11: Spencer Backman, Matthew Baker, and Chi Ho Yuen,
*Geometric Bijections for Regular Matroids, Zonotopes, and Ehrhart Theory*,
[arXiv:1701.01051, PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1701.01051),
Section 2.1, Lemma 2.1.1, printed page 9.

The preceding definition specifies full row rank, integral entries, TU,
and an ordinary matroid represented over the rationals. The lemma gives
an integral unimodular row transformation and column permutations/signs
between TU representations of the same matroid.

Its wording allows permutation. The
[local proof](../proof/regular-representation-independence.md) separately
derives the labeled statement after aligning a prescribed matroid
isomorphism, then applies one witness to every modulus. Rank zero is
treated locally. No arbitrary arithmetic representation-equivalence
result or unique canonical comparison map is inferred.

This is a standard background theorem, not a novelty claim. The source
locator supports provenance; the repository's integral argument and
explicit scope support its claim record.
